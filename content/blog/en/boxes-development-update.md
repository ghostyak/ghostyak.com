**Boxes is still being developed.** Our current focus is making its existing desktop features more stable.

## Focusing on stability

A desktop organizer is part of the workspace you use every day. Organizing files and shortcuts and working with boxes should feel dependable.

That is the principle guiding our ongoing work. This post is a progress update, not an announcement that stabilization is complete.

## More development updates to come

We will share completed changes in future updates. If you encounter a problem or see room for improvement, please tell us what was happening when it occurred.

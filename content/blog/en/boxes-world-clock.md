**A world clock widget has been added to Boxes.** You can now check the time in multiple cities on the same desktop where you organize your files and shortcuts.

## The world’s time, side by side

Morning in Seoul, early hours in London, night in New York. At the same moment, cities can have different times and dates. The world clock widget displays information for multiple cities side by side, making local times easy to compare.

- Compare **city times** at a glance.
- See the **date and day of the week** to distinguish regions on a different day.
- Check each city’s time zone difference with its **UTC offset**.

## Check your desktop before reaching out

Before messaging a colleague abroad or calling family far away, check the time in their city. It is a small, useful tool for people who work or stay in touch across regions.

Use it alongside the existing file, folder, and shortcut organization features to keep the items you need and the world’s time in one workspace.

## Try it in Boxes

Boxes is available for Windows 10/11 and is free to download. Visit the product page to explore the world clock preview and key features.

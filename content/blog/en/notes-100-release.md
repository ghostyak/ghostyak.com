**Ghostyak Notes v1.0.0 is released.** Thank you to everyone who used the beta through October. This release includes everything that landed between beta v0.1.5 and v0.1.13.

## From beta to a 30-day trial

The release is a trial: every feature is available for 30 days from the day you first launch it. When the period ends you can still open and read documents, find within a document and export to PDF with your handwriting, but writing and searching all documents are no longer available. Your documents and handwriting are not deleted.

Unlike the beta, installing a new version does not extend the period. Installing v1.0.0 on a PC that ran the beta starts a fresh 30-day trial.

To keep going you need Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase, and one key activates up to 3 devices. Purchasing is being built and will be available soon. See [Compare Trial and Pro](/en/product/notes#pricing) for details.

## New home screen

The library home has been redesigned. The brand color is now navy, and the app icon is new.

- The app opens on the **Recent** view, and the **Continue reading** shelf at the top opens the document you were reading, right where you left off.
- The **Bookmarks** and **Marked text** views on the home screen gather bookmarks and marked text from every document, grouped by document, and each document can be collapsed and expanded.
- Search on the home screen is split by the view you are in (Recent, All documents, folders and so on).
- Drag folders and documents to move or delete them, including documents that are open. Select several cards with Shift and Ctrl.
- When there are many folders, folder cards show up to two rows, and folders in the left list can be collapsed.

## Merging documents and new notes

- Select several documents in the library and merge them into one.
- Press **+** on the tab bar to create a new note right away.
- Drop several PDFs at once and they are added to the library without opening each one.

## Writing and marking

- Tool settings are now tabs. Each slot has its own color and thickness, and you can pick your own colors. Switch tools with the number keys 1 to 7.
- Shapes get a line palette, opacity, no outline and custom colors. Resize a selection with the edge handles; Shift and Ctrl keep the proportions.
- Text in text boxes can be highlighted, underlined, struck through or covered. A cover can be made see-through for a moment with **Translucent** in its menu.
- The eraser also erases text boxes.
- The menus for selected text and marks have been merged into one.

## OCR

- Images placed on a page are recognized too, so you can select and find text on them.
- Next to Find on the toolbar there are now **OCR this page** and **OCR whole document** buttons.
- Automatic OCR can be turned on and off in Settings, and it uses less CPU when it is on.

## Minimap and navigation

- The app now draws its own scrollbar, so it shows the whole document in single- and two-page views, and it sits to the right of the minimap.
- The minimap shows where outline entries really are, and you can choose outline levels (1, 2, 3). Scrolling the wheel over the minimap moves the strip without moving the document.
- Links inside the original PDF are followed, and the mouse back and forward buttons move between places.
- The toolbar gains a page field and a rotate page button, and the zoom steps include the fit-to-page level.
- Change thumbnail size in the page overview and the sidebar with Ctrl+wheel. When there are many tabs, they shrink to an equal width.
- Shortcuts have been reorganized: F4 or Ctrl+B for the left sidebar, F9 for the page overview, and Alt+1 to 8 for the sidebar tabs.

## Also

- Newly added documents are found even while the index is being rebuilt.
- The splash screen appears faster and only stays on the first launch.
- Fixed the document jumping while dragging the minimap's wide bar, the view shifting when toggling the cover page, and more.

It is a full release, but there is still plenty to polish. If anything gets in your way, please let us know.

**We have released Ghostyak Notes, a PDF note-taking app for Windows.**

Open a PDF and write directly on it, mark it up with the highlighter and underlines, and quickly find the place you need with bookmarks and search.

It is a beta for now. Try it and tell us what you think, and we will bring your feedback into the next version.
